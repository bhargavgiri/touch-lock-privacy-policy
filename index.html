infoAbout the app

Touch Lock is built to keep the display active while blocking accidental touches

The app is designed for situations where users want to keep content visible but prevent unwanted taps. This includes videos, maps, recipe screens, exercise apps, children's content, and other everyday scenarios where the screen should stay on without accidental interaction.

  • One tap touch lock for fast activation.
  • Floating or notification-based unlock support where available.
  • Screen-on behavior for hands-free viewing and touch protection.
  • Material-style interface with settings and optional advanced controls.

lockPermissions disclosure

Permissions are requested only for app functionality, stability, and supported services

Touch Lock uses a limited set of Android permissions to deliver touch-blocking features, keep the service active when needed, support notifications, and enable ad-related or analytics-related connectivity. Users can review and manage permissions through Android settings.

Data safety

Touch Lock is intended to provide its main protection features without needing access to private communications or uploaded personal content. The functional purpose of the app is screen-touch control, not message reading or content surveillance.

  • No private chats or messages are described as required for touch lock features.
  • No call audio recording is listed as part of the app's purpose.
  • No personal information is stated as being collected for the core utility function.
  • Most feature behavior is expected to remain device-side.
